About

Concept is a UK-based electronic artist and producer operating at the intersection of techno, industrial soundscapes and hypnotic rhythm. Equal parts DJ, live performer and studio architect. Influenced by the warehouses of Berlin and the rolling underground of Manchester, the sound is singular — heavy, warm and relentless. Built for 4am floors.
